Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D

Age Suitability

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Applies to Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D (com.canalgrid.WoodUltraSmooth3D) on iOS and Android, published by Canal Grid

Suitable for everyone

Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D is a calm, single-player craft game. You shape a spinning piece of wood on a lathe, sand it smooth, paint it and keep it in your collection. There is nothing in the game that is frightening, violent, sexual or rude.

Intended for all ages and submitted with the lowest age rating on the App Store (4+) and Google Play (Everyone). The game is played offline, has no accounts, no chat and no way to meet other people.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and reward videos that the player chooses to watch for a bonus. Ads come from Google AdMob under Google's family-safe advertising policies; we do not sell ad space ourselves and never choose individual ads.

  • Reward ads only play when the player taps a clearly labelled button.
  • On iOS the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Declining it keeps the game fully playable; ads simply become less personalised.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off banners and full-screen ads permanently.

In-app purchases

The game can be played from start to finish for free. Optional purchases are a one-time Remove Ads and packs of in-game coins, priced from US$0.99 to US$8.99. All purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so the device's own parental controls and purchase password apply:

  • iOS: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases → Don't Allow
  • Android: Google Play → Settings → Authentication → Require authentication for purchases

Coins can also be earned simply by playing, and there is no timer, energy system or pressure to spend money to continue.

Tips for parents

  • The game works fully offline. Turning off Wi-Fi or mobile data removes all ads.
  • Nothing a child does in the game can be seen by anyone else, and nobody can contact them through it.
  • Sound and vibration can be switched off in the game's Settings.
  • If you have any concern about age suitability, email us and we will answer within 30 days.

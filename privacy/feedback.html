Before you write

Three things solve most reports on the spot:

  • Cannot reach the target shape? Work in passes rather than one long cut, and switch to a finer grit before the final sanding stage — the smoothness score is measured against the guide outline, not against how fast you got there.
  • A purchase is missing? Open the settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Google Play account and is the fix in almost every case, including after reinstalling or changing phone.
  • The game runs slowly or gets hot? Lower Graphics quality in settings. It changes how the shavings and reflections are drawn, and nothing about how the game plays.

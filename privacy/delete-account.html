Delete Your Data

Last updated: 25 August 2026
Applies to Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D (com.canalgrid.WoodUltraSmooth3D), published by Canal Grid

This game has no accounts

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. We never create an account for you, and we hold no profile, no server-side save, and no personal information about you anywhere.

Everything the game remembers is stored on your own phone, which means you can delete all of it yourself, immediately, without asking us.

Option 1 — Delete everything (uninstall)

Uninstalling the app permanently erases every piece of data it created.

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps and select Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D
  3. Tap Uninstall, then confirm

You can also press and hold the game's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall. Once it is removed the data is gone — there is no backup on our side to restore it from, so this cannot be undone.

One thing survives, on purpose: any in-app purchase you made stays attached to your Google Play account, not to the app. If you reinstall later, tap Restore Purchases in the settings screen and it comes back. Deleting the app never costs you something you paid for.

Option 2 — Reset your progress but keep the game

If you would rather start over with a clean workshop without removing the app:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps and select Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D
  3. Tap Storage
  4. Tap Clear data (shown as Clear storage on some devices), then confirm

The app stays installed, and every project, star, record and unlocked tool resets to the very beginning. Purchases are unaffected — use Restore Purchases to bring them back.

Option 3 — Ask us to do it

You do not need our help to delete anything, but you are welcome to ask us to confirm it in writing. This is also the route to take if you believe we hold data about you.

  1. Email vmwnpela@gmail.com with the subject Account and data deletion request
  2. Tell us the name of the app (Woodturning Ultra Smooth 3D) and, if you like, the device you play on
  3. We reply within 30 days. In almost every case the reply confirms that we hold no data that identifies you, and that removing the app is all that is required.

Retention period: we operate no server and no database, so we hold no copy of your progress for any length of time. The only records connected to the app sit with the third parties listed above, under their own retention rules.

About crash reports and diagnostics

Crash reports and broad gameplay events are collected by Firebase Crashlytics and Unity Analytics so that we can fix problems. They contain no name, email address or anything else that identifies you, which also means there is no record we could look up and delete on request — there is nothing tying those reports to you in the first place.

You can read how each provider handles this data, and what controls they offer, here:

  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Unity game player and app user privacy policy

About the advertising identifier

The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it works with. To select and measure ads, those companies process your device's advertising identifier. That identifier belongs to your device and is controlled by you, not by us — we never receive it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

You can reset or remove it yourself at any time:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Privacy, then Ads (the exact wording varies by device)
  3. Choose Delete advertising ID, or Reset advertising ID to start fresh

To ask those companies directly about data they hold, see the Unity / ironSource privacy policy.

About purchases

Purchases are recorded by Google Play against your Google account, and we never receive your payment details. We cannot delete a purchase record on your behalf, and deleting one is not something you would normally want — it is what lets you restore the purchase on a new phone. Purchase history, receipts and refunds are managed in the Play Store and under the Google Privacy Policy.
